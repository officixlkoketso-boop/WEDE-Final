About us

Limpopo Fresh Bakes is a small bakery in Limpopo. The bakery makes birthday cakes, cupcakes and custom orders for local customers.

The website helps customers see the products, prices and contact details before making an enquiry.

Our goal

Our goal is to provide fresh baked products and friendly service for birthdays, celebrations and family events.
